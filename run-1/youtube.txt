=== DESCRIPTION ===
In July eighteen ninety-seven, news of a ton of Alaskan gold reached Seattle and sparked the wild Klondike Gold Rush. Over one hundred thousand men packed up their lives and rushed into the frozen Yukon wilderness, facing impossible mountain passes, starvation, and lethal rapids.

This is the true story of Walt Becker and his partner Sam, two ordinary men who carried a ton of gear up the legendary Golden Stairs of Chilkoot Pass and built a wooden boat to braved the wild North-West territory.
