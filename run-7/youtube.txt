=== DESCRIPTION ===
In July 1897, news reached Seattle that a ton of gold had been pulled from the frozen Yukon ground. Some one hundred thousand people packed up their lives and rushed north into the freezing wilderness, unprepared for the brutal winter ahead.

Join old prospector Walt Becker by the cabin wood stove as he recalls the grueling climb up the Chilkoot Pass, the strict Mounted Police laws, the roaring rapids of Miles Canyon, and the harsh reality waiting at Dawson City.
